# Project Specification

## Working Title

Agentic AI for Customer Experience Intelligence:
A Multi-Branch CRM, Data Mining and Predictive Analytics Study of a Hyderabad Mandi Restaurant Brand

## Domain

Mandi restaurant / food-service industry.

## Unit of Analysis

Customer review.

Secondary analytical unit:

Restaurant branch.

Primary organizational unit:

One restaurant brand.

## CRM Objective

Identify customer pain points, branch-level experience gaps, emerging issues and recommended CRM actions.

## Data Mining Objective

Discover sentiment patterns, topics, clusters, associations and anomalies in customer feedback.

## Predictive Analytics Objective

Forecast customer-feedback trends and identify branches/issues at elevated risk of deterioration.

## Agentic AI Objective

Allow an AI orchestrator to combine analytical tools and produce evidence-based CRM insights and recommendations.

## User Interface

Streamlit dashboard.

## Initial Data

Real Google Maps review export stored locally as JSON.

## Data Integrity

Real, derived, predicted and synthetic data must be explicitly distinguished.
